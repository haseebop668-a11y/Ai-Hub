# Curriculum source and edition policy

## Punjab baseline

Use the official PECTAA Books and Publications and Curriculum & Compliance pages:
- https://pectaa.edu.pk/books-and-publications/
- https://pectaa.edu.pk/curriculum-compliance/

The official Grade IX listings include 2025-26 editions for English, Urdu, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Computer. The separate Curriculum & Compliance listing identifies **Computer and Entrepreneur 9 (2026-27)**. For a 2026-27 Punjab learner, do not use the older 2020/2023-24 Computer Science book as the current textbook. The exact linked PDF and edition must be checked before importing or authoring chapter content.\n\nOfficial linked 2026-27 Computer and Entrepreneur Grade 9 (English Medium):\n- https://drive.google.com/file/d/1C8pGmjCU0swY_N4fhpeVcx_-0oRrEss3/view?usp=drive_link\n\nThe PECTAA e-book index is the current authority for the other Grade IX titles and edition labels. It lists English, Urdu, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and Biology 2025-26 books separately; each selected PDF must be matched to the student's current board/year before content ingestion.

## Federal baseline

Use the National Curriculum Council's Rationalized Curricula 2026 Grade 9-12 page:
- https://ncc.gov.pk/Detail/YmFhODM2NTQtMmI1Mi00MGE5LWE3YTUtNTM4Y2U3ZGY4OGJi

Federal curriculum documents are maintained as a distinct profile. Do not assume Punjab textbooks, chapter order, paper patterns or assessment schemes are interchangeable with Federal Board requirements.

## Content provenance fields

Every authored lesson, explanation, solved question and flashcard must record:
- curriculum authority
- board/profile
- academic year
- subject and medium
- textbook title and edition
- chapter/section/page reference
- source URL or user-imported document identifier
- reviewer and verification status

Unverified or generated material must never be labelled as an official board answer or past-paper question. A past-paper item requires the actual paper year, board and question reference.

## Textbook distribution

Do not commit copyrighted textbook PDFs or commercial font/model assets unless redistribution terms explicitly permit it. Prefer user-imported local books or assets with a documented redistribution licence.
