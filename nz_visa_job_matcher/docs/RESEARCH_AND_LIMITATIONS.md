# New Zealand recruitment and AEWV research

Research checked on **9 November 2026**. Immigration settings and website terms can change; re-check official sources before relying on a job or making a visa decision.

## Immigration requirements that affect screening

The Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) requires a current full-time offer from an accredited employer, at least 30 hours a week, an approved job check for the specific job and pay at least the market rate. Applicants must also meet the role's skill, qualification, experience, English (where applicable), health and character requirements.

INZ's standard minimum skill evidence generally includes two years of relevant work experience or a qualification at NZQCF level 4 or higher, subject to exceptions. A CV alone is not sufficient proof of the work-experience requirement. Keep independently verifiable employment records, payslips, tax evidence and qualification documents.

Official sources:
- Accredited Employer Work Visa: https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visas/accredited-employer-work-visa/
- AEWV employer accreditation and job check process: https://www.immigration.govt.nz/work/for-employers/getting-accreditation-or-approval-to-hire/employer-accreditation-for-the-aewv/aewv-employer-accreditation-and-job-check-process/
- Official accredited employer search: https://www.immigration.govt.nz/work/requirements-for-work-visas/approved-employers/accredited-employer-list/

The official employer search is updated daily, but some accredited employers elect not to appear publicly. Failure to find an employer is therefore not conclusive proof that it is not accredited. Confirm directly with the employer and use its legal name or NZBN.

Accreditation is employer-level. It does not prove that a particular vacancy has a valid approved job check. The candidate should ask the employing entity to confirm the job check for the exact role, location, hours and employment arrangement.

## Website automation boundary

SEEK's New Zealand terms updated 20 July 2026 prohibit data mining, robots, screen scraping and automated data collection without prior written consent. The terms also restrict automated bots and applying for jobs on behalf of another party. The app therefore opens SEEK in the user's browser and supports manual import only.

For all other sites, the app does not assume that public HTML access grants scraping or automated-submission permission. Integrations require a documented API/feed or written permission. CAPTCHA and OTP steps remain human-controlled; the app does not bypass verification. Push alerts for OTP/CAPTCHA are not included in this local-only first build.

## Job classification

- **Likely local-only restriction:** explicit wording such as 'NZ citizens only', 'must have NZ residency', 'valid NZ work visa required' or 'no visa sponsorship'.
- **Potential AEWV lead:** wording such as 'AEWV candidates welcome', 'visa sponsorship', 'overseas applicants welcome' or relocation support. These are signals, not proof.
- **Conflicting wording — review:** both positive sponsorship language and a work-rights restriction are present.
- **Sponsorship status unknown:** insufficient evidence.

The app deliberately does not mark a job as verified solely because a keyword appears. Its evidence checklist records the user's confirmation that the employer is currently accredited, that the employer has confirmed a job check for this vacancy, that at least 30 guaranteed hours are offered, and the source/date of that confirmation. This remains a user-entered checklist, not an INZ decision.

## Applicant-protection warning

INZ says New Zealand employers or their agents cannot charge workers recruitment costs or fees for a job. Do not pay an employer, recruiter, intermediary or family member acting for an employer to secure an offer. Confirm unexpected offers using the employer's official website and contact details, and request a genuine video or in-person interview.
